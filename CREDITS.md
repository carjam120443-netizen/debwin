## Package licensing

The ISO contains many additional Debian packages and their dependencies. The authoritative
copyright and license information for installed Debian packages is kept by Debian's package
metadata, normally under `/usr/share/doc/<package>/copyright`.

Do not remove or replace those upstream notices when redistributing debwin.
